# Northeastern AI and Business Administration: concentration review

Reviewed 2026-10-10 against the 2026–2027 official catalog. The [AI/Business degree](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/undergraduate/computer-information-science/computer-information-science-combined-majors/artificial-intelligence-business-admnistration-bs-bos/) requires **at least one business concentration** from its own linked list. That list, rather than the larger school navigation menu, establishes applicability to this particular degree. The 14 linked concentration requirement tabs were fetched using the production robots-aware Fetcher and reviewed. No family definition or other variant was changed.

Thirteen concentrations are eligible for the required first selection. International Business is available **only as a second concentration** and has a separate optional conditional subtree. The required selection is an alternative group whose summary preserves “at least one”; it does not prohibit further concentrations. All concentration course inventories apply only if that concentration is selected. They are not additional universal degree requirements.
