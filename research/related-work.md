# Related research

Reviewed 11 October 2026.

Felix Muzny and colleagues' [A Tool to Map AI Programs in the U.S.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12428v2) reports an April 2026 snapshot of 361 undergraduate AI programs across a 563-institution search. Its program types include certificates. The August 20 revision is the version inspected here. The paper and [CIC interactive map](https://cicmap.ai/) are relevant prior work, not evidence that this atlas is the first or most comprehensive survey.

The map's visible methodology focuses on technical AI programs within computing units, with institutions selected using CS graduate counts; it describes automated extraction and format limitations. Its population, title/type decisions and snapshot differ from this project's separate ranked cohorts and inclusion of relevant non-computing units. Counts must not be presented as directly comparable. Its visible footer restricts commercial reuse; no map dataset, course inventory or implementation was imported into this repository. Any future individual lead requires independent official-source verification under the atlas contract.
