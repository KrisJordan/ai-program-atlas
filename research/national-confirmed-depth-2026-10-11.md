## Interpretation and access

Rice’s exact title remains **AI & Systems**. Its declared BSECE area is a formal curricular path with finite requirements, but the registrar explicitly distinguishes it from an official credential or transcript-recorded major concentration. Stanford G4 core implementation in Fall 2020 and September 2024 document updates are not program launch evidence. Northwestern additional majors do not grant separate degrees.

Public Google Docs export was used only after the ordinary viewer expressly showed anyone-with-link, no-sign-in access. HTTP document shells are not course evidence. The Stanford-login approved-nominations sheet was not accessed. Columbia’s denied central-directory route and Cornell’s previously challenged catalog routes were not retried. Duke’s published crawl delay was respected.

The five records provide actual observed course inventories, nested selection rules, open eligibility criteria, scoped policies and manual partial audits. No host-degree outcomes are relabeled as local-path outcomes. Unknown credits and course metadata remain missing. Tests use synthetic fixtures and optional observed-source replay.
