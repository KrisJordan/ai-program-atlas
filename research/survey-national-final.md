# National Universities: final first-pass supplement

Checked 2026-10-10. This supplement covers the 16 previously pending National Universities assigned for a first inspection attempt. Nine institutions have qualifying undergraduate program identities confirmed in readable official sources; five were partially inspected without a qualifying program confirmed; two were inaccessible. No institution-wide absence claims are made. This is a first pass of the loaded cohort, not an exhaustive inventory of offerings or confirmation of the complete ranking cohort.

Twelve new program records are promoted with partial verification and curriculum pending: two degrees, four minors, and six concentrations/specializations. Formal emphasis and specialization titles are represented as concentrations. Detailed requirements, outcomes, first-offered dates, and academic calendars were not extracted or inferred.

## Specific interpretation limits

- William & Mary’s Applied AI major awards a BA in Interdisciplinary Studies. Its CS BS AI/ML concentration and Data Science BS AI specialization belong to different parent majors and remain separate records.
- Stony Brook’s current admissions directory lists Artificial Intelligence and Data Science under the CS BS. The readable Fall 2024 official catalog establishes a declarable specialization; current catalog detail was inaccessible. The record does not claim current curriculum verification.
- Minnesota’s approved AI and Machine Learning minor is announced for 2027. The program page and older announcement differ in timing detail; first-offered remains unknown.
- Rutgers names an Artificial Intelligence and Cognitive Science elective track, but inspected guidance did not establish a formal credential or transcript-recognized concentration. It remains an unpromoted lead.
- Washington’s official undergraduate page mentions an upcoming AI minor via a news summary. Exact formal program identity and approval were not established in the inspected pages; this remains a lead.
- Florida State review covered the minors directory, GW the CS department bulletin, and UMass the computing undergraduate directory. Their institution-wide program inventories remain incomplete.
- Brandeis and Pittsburgh attempts produced access errors. These attempts are recorded as inaccessible, never as evidence of no program. William & Mary and Stony Brook also had inaccessible detailed catalog links, but other readable official sources supported their identities.

## Adapter behavior

Each promoted identity has a source-specific adapter. Refresh requires the observed main-content heading and program/classification phrases; navigation-only mentions, missing claims, and login/challenge pages are rejected. Parsers emit only identity fields and never replay a stored curriculum or manufacture verification timestamps. Full curriculum extraction remains a separate outstanding task.
