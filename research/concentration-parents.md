# Concentration parent programs — source review

Reviewed 2026-10-11. All 50 existing concentration-kind records were individually reviewed for parent title, award and relationship. This is a bounded identity review, not certification of any host degree curriculum. Result: 47 verified, two partial award assignments, and one university-wide immersion with no individual parent verified. Ohio State has two actual parents; the total is 50 parent relationships across 49 records.

Evidence was read from normal official HTTP responses and their local cached originals, plus the existing public rendered program fragments for American and San Diego. Eight independently accessible official pages were fetched again through the robots-aware Fetcher on 2026-10-11 (03:24 UTC). Existing source fetch dates are preserved; review dates do not pretend every page was freshly fetched. Restricted catalogs were not retried or bypassed. Raw pages remain uncommitted.

Full award labels are retained where observed; an unobserved abbreviation is null. No parent is inferred from a department name, course prefix, optional double major, shared AI title or neighboring degree listing. The abbreviation BA in CMU Business Administration prose is not treated as Bachelor of Arts.
