# National Universities upper-tail survey

Checked 2026-10-10. This ledger covers all 27 institutions ranked above 60 through 100 (including ties) that were still pending in `national.json` at assignment. Existing nonpending institutions and pilots were deliberately left unchanged. The machine-readable evidence is in `data/surveys/national-upper-tail.json`.

## Results and limits

- 6 institutions have confirmed qualifying titles, totaling 9 discoveries. These are title-level findings, not completed curriculum extraction.
- 20 institutions have partial inspections of official catalogs or directories. No institution-wide absence is asserted.
- UConn's official undergraduate catalog returned a JavaScript robot-verification challenge. Access was not bypassed.
- The search provider returned unrelated generic results during this pass; evidence instead comes from direct official pages and links followed within them. Failed guessed or moved URLs were not treated as evidence of absence or final inaccessibility when a working official route was available.
- Dynamic directory results are incomplete at Fordham, Creighton, Rutgers–Camden and San Diego. Their notes identify the exact limitation.
- Delaware confirms a major and Marquette an additional major requiring another primary business major; their precise bachelor award labels are unverified (`other` in the discovery ledger). No launch dates are inferred from page publication or discovery dates.
- Drexel's official degree page states BS, quarter calendar and first admittance in Fall 2026; the Fall 2025 date refers to applications. Missouri's engineering AI page explicitly excludes an undergraduate AI degree, but this does not establish university-wide absence of minors or concentrations. Saint Louis's AI MS pathways and microcredential are excluded from the current degree/minor/concentration scope.

## Implementation and checks

Nine per-program adapters observe identity only. Six use the shared discovery identity checks directly or with major context; the three emphasis/specialization adapters additionally validate the named parent BS and a scoped list. No parser replays the curated seed, timestamps or curriculum. `tests/test_upper_tail_discoveries.py` supplies 16 synthetic checks for identity, missing awards, wrong sections, unrelated parent programs and navigation-only false positives. Live raw-source parser replay remains a separate network orchestration check; the research itself inspected rendered official pages.
