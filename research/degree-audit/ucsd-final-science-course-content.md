## Manual content boundaries

- **Foundations:** DSC 20 teaches Python abstraction, functions, objects, recursion and basic data structures; DSC 30 teaches Java programming, abstract data types, algorithms and data structures. Their data-science setting does not automatically make them AI. CSS 1 explicitly teaches Python and computational problem formulation, in its stated 2023–24 edition.
- **Mathematics:** MATH 20D, 20E and 111A teach differential equations, vector calculus and mathematical modeling. MATLAB, engineering examples and varied social/scientific applications support the mathematical methods, not a separate computing or unknown-subject classification.
- **Human context:** MATH 163 studies mathematical history and original sources rather than mathematical technique. ANBI 176 analyzes Indigenous knowledge systems in climate discourse; technical measurement or environmental-engineering instruction is not separately stated. ANBI 174 critically watches and discusses conservation films using readings; “Film Lab” does not establish filmmaking practice.
- **Applications:** ANBI 130 centers physiological and molecular consequences/measurement of inequality. Social inequality is the causal context rather than a separately developed political-analysis curriculum. COGS 2 studies neural and cognitive mechanisms behind economic decisions, not economic policy or mathematical economic theory.
- **Mixed applications/human context:** ANBI 131 explicitly combines human genetics/evolution with social constructions of race and racism. ANBI 132 explicitly combines biological conservation with cultural, historical, economic, political and ethical perspectives. ANBI 121 combines biology, geography and navigation science with historical/anthropological accounts of Polynesian voyaging. DSC 160 joins arts practice and critical questions of authorship, representation, visualization and provenance. Its data-science methods are insufficiently specified to add an AI/ML or mathematics category.
- **Unclassified:** DSC 180A/180B describe a data-project lifecycle, proposal/design and implementation/evaluation, but do not establish actual project domains or modeling methods. Statistical output and system design alone do not certify a dominant AI/ML, mathematics, software or applied subject. COGS 190A refines an honors project/proposal whose substantive topic remains unspecified. Detailed project logistics are not a substitute for actual content.

ANBI 132's older pool calls it cross-listed with BIEB 176, but neither current block independently restates that relationship. The separately reviewed BIEB 176 description emphasizes biological conservation and has a different prerequisite, whereas ANBI 132 expressly includes social/ethical perspectives. The present difference is preserved; the old label does not justify collapsing their classifications or eligibility.
