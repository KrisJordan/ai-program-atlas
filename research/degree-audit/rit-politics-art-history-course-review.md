## Interpretation and conditions

All 62 descriptions support human-context, with original summaries and individualized rationales. This is a source-content result, not a classification by department or pool membership.

- POLS-215/230/295/370 analyze technology through ethics, law, political participation and conflict. AI, cyberattacks and robotics are subjects of analysis, not evidence that these courses teach technical AI or security methods.
- POLS-290/384/415 use biological or evolutionary accounts to examine political behavior, international relations and law. POLS-420 describes great-ape political structures to interpret human political behavior; its political explanatory purpose is retained. No laboratory-science or computing curriculum is inferred.
- POLS-220 examines state-market relations through political traditions and ethical questions; its political-economy title does not trigger the applied-economic-model category used for different actual ECON descriptions.
- POLS-300 explicitly includes rhetorical analysis and disciplinary writing. POLS-430 teaches legal interpretation, briefing and argument. Practical communication does not become generic applications under the established taxonomy.
- POLS-375 has an instructor-announced regime/area focus and may be repeated when that focus changes. POLS-380 lists possible topics, not a guaranteed syllabus. POLS-541/542 list Summer as typical, without establishing current enrollment or campus eligibility.
- ARTH-400 requires ARTH-124, ARTH-135, ARTH-136 or equivalent. Prior 300-level-or-higher art history is **strongly recommended**, not made mandatory. The research topic is announced by the instructor.
- ARTH-550/551 permit repeats with different topics. ARTH-555 prohibits repeating the same topic. These remain topic-specific rules rather than inferred finite content.
- ARTH-551 explicitly limits availability to RIT degree-seeking undergraduates; that condition is preserved in notes separately from the absent prerequisite field.
- ARTH-522 studies documentary aesthetics, ARTH-556 interprets comics historically, and ARTH-584 interprets design history. Their descriptions do not establish studio production requirements.
- ARTH-568 explicitly prioritizes historical, theoretical and ideological relationships between art and technology. ARTH-588 studies psychoanalytic theory as art interpretation, not clinical practice.

No course-identity groups, compulsory AI requirements, route promotions or whole-degree certifications are created by this contribution. Local route selection, campus and prerequisite-role rules remain the primary owner’s responsibility.
