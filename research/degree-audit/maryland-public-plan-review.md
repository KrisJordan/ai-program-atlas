## Source facts

- Page 1 explicitly labels benchmarks: HCAI 100 by the end of two semesters;
  HCAI 200 and INST 204 by the end of four; HCAI 301 and one upper-level HCAI
  specialization course by the end of six.
- The plan includes MATH 115, with an attempt by 30 credits. Page 2 lists it
  under mathematics and alongside technical coursework. The catalog separately
  names it among the college requirements in addition to the 16-course major.
  It should not disappear into an unclassified requirement-role note. The plan's
  18-credit technical-core label is not a new permission to add MATH 115 to that
  subtotal; the catalog's six technical courses already total 18 credits.
- Page 2 says HCAI 121 and HCAI 200 require MATH 115 or higher as preparation.
  This explicit prerequisite statement does not by itself establish a blanket
  substitution for the catalog's separately named MATH 115 degree condition.
- The specialization footnote permits proposing a custom specialization for
  individual consideration. It does not approve a proposal or identify additional
  finite course choices. The seven catalog tracks remain the named inventory.
- The ordinary four-year layout uses ENGL 101 as its writing example; it is not
  evidence that all university-approved writing alternatives disappear.
- The plan prints HCAI 490. The current catalog expressly allows an internship
  replacement, so the plan does not make HCAI 490 universally fixed.
- Total 120 degree credits and 39 upper-level credits, ordinary university
  residence/upper-major requirements, conditional ARHU 158 or equivalent, and
  Global Engagement agree with the current catalog. The ARHU website separately
  states 39 upper-level credits effective for fall-2024 college entrants; its
  historical 45-credit statement should retain that cohort distinction.
