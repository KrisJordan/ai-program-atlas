Sources: the 2026–2027 [English descriptions](https://bulletin.case.edu/course-descriptions/engl/) and [Art History descriptions](https://bulletin.case.edu/course-descriptions/arth/), captured with permitted HTTP 200 responses. Raw documents remain outside public commits.

ARTH 396's junior/senior timing is advisory, not an invented mandatory prerequisite. ARTH 390A's internship consideration applies to the named graduate sections, not automatically the undergraduate record.

ENGL 266's heading is 266 while its body lists 365F aliases. Preserve the heading identity and flag the mismatch rather than inventing a cross-list group. ENGL 250 and ARTH 220 have explicit cross-list statements consistent with previously reviewed counterparts; identity-group additions still require the primary reviewer's reciprocal checks. Distinct capstone/non-capstone identities are not merged merely because both are listed in an Offered as sentence or have identical descriptions.

This is a bounded course-content contribution. The primary reviewer integrates source adapters, requirements and the audit ledger; it does not certify eligibility of every listed cross-disciplinary capstone or the entire AI degree.
