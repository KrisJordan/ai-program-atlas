# RIT eleven statistics cards — 2026-10-11

Read all eleven STAT course popups linked from the current
[Applied Statistics Immersion](https://www.rit.edu/study/applied-statistics-immersion)
table after its 2026–2027 rules were reviewed. The five MATH preparation/choice
rows already have separately reviewed mathematics evidence and were not expanded
again. This contribution does not certify the whole route or degree and does not
recursively add prerequisite courses.

All eleven actual cards publish three credits and explicit prerequisite text.
The manifest preserves the published Boolean expressions, equivalent-course
clauses and credit exclusions. STAT-205's engineering audience/calculus condition
is not a claim that every AI student automatically qualifies. STAT-257 excludes
credit combinations with MATH-252 and STAT-205. These are enrollment/credit
conditions, not additional fixed degree obligations or alias relations.

Ten descriptions primarily teach statistical concepts or methods. STAT-330
instead centers on data-visualization design, visual encoding and communication
of data features using R; it is reviewed as applications. Its institutional
mathematical-perspective GE attribute is preserved without overriding that
content-based judgment. STAT-325 explicitly has both WI-GE and WI-Program
attributes, but the contribution makes no blanket current-term WI availability
claim. Semester-hour normalization uses the separately reviewed D01.4 policy,
not merely the credit label on a card.

The observation parser requires the exact route/year/rendered table and a settled,
bound statistics card. It extracts current description, prerequisite and credit
facts, but emits no manual review, audit or stale curriculum requirements. Tests
exercise changed fields, identity/version/body failures and the manual scope.
They are synthetic fixtures, not a claimed replay of exported live HTML. The
ordinary public UI evidence was read directly; no hidden catalog endpoint or
restricted search was used. The manual contribution remains separate from seed
integration until the full route queue is assembled.
