# RIT psychology, criminology and leadership course cards

Reviewed 2026-10-11. This contribution completes the reserved **54 actual course-card records**: 21 PSYC, 17 CRIM and 16 LEAD. Every complete popup was opened through its public table link and read, including its actual description, credit amount, prerequisite field when published, contact hours, attributes and offering information. All 54 were accessible. No hidden endpoint, prerequisite-chain expansion or alternative access mechanism was used.

The six exact source pages display **2026–2027** curricula:

- [Psychology Immersion](https://www.rit.edu/study/psychology-immersion): 20 assigned PSYC cards. Already reviewed PSYC-101 and PSYC-223 were excluded.
- [Computational Linguistics Immersion](https://www.rit.edu/study/computational-linguistics-immersion): PSYC-431 only.
- [Criminal Justice Immersion](https://www.rit.edu/study/criminal-justice-immersion): 14 assigned CRIM cards.
- [Legal Studies Immersion](https://www.rit.edu/study/legal-studies-immersion): CRIM-215, CRIM-225 and CRIM-315.
- [Deaf Leadership Immersion](https://www.rit.edu/study/deaf-leadership-immersion): nine LEAD cards.
- [Diversity, Inclusion, and Dialogue Immersion](https://www.rit.edu/study/diversity-inclusion-and-dialogue-immersion): LEAD-203, LEAD-304 and LEAD-309 through LEAD-313.

Each of the 54 cards independently prints **(3 Credits)**. No value comes from a route total. Semester-hour normalization cites the separately reviewed [D01.4 policy](https://www.rit.edu/policies/d014); the observer leaves the unit unknown because the card itself says Credits. PSYC-255 separately prints **Contact Hours: Lecture 1**. The three-credit amount and one-hour contact measure are both retained, with no invented reconciliation.

Exactly 24 cards publish prerequisite text: all assigned PSYC cards except PSYC-101H, plus CRIM-210, CRIM-265, CRIM-290 and CRIM-489. The other 30 are source-unreported, not unrestricted. Every LEAD card in this batch lacks a prerequisite field. Exact wording is preserved, including:

- Regular PSYC-221 permits introductory psychology or completion of a 200-level PSYC course; its honors counterpart instead names introductory psychology or an equivalent course.
- PSYC-224H additionally names honors status or a cumulative GPA of at least 3.3. The published and/or sentence is retained without inventing stronger parenthesization.
- PSYC-255 requires PSYC-101 and STAT-145 or MATH-251, or equivalent courses, with the source parentheses intact.
- PSYC-300 requires at least one PSYC course at 200 level or higher; repeat enrollment cannot repeat the same topic.
- PSYC-431 publishes its PSYC-223, PSYC-251/255 and STAT-145 preparation exactly. Its description says it is intended for the cognitive track, which is not an explicit exclusion of other qualified students.

Manual subject decisions are **27 human-context, 19 applications, five mixed and three unclassified**. Each has an original summary and individual rationale; none follows a department prefix alone. Empirical psychology mechanisms and behavioral research are domain applications. Social and ethical interpretation determines PSYC-231. PSYC-237 and PSYC-242 explicitly combine cultural inquiry with psychology-domain content and remain mixed. A statistics prerequisite does not automatically make PSYC-255 mathematics.

CRIM-510 discusses AI governance, law, policy and trust practices rather than algorithms. PSYC-431 includes AI as a discussion topic within cognition and language, not as a documented AI-methods course. CRIM-290 analyzes computer crime and its operational, legal and ethical context without establishing hands-on security implementation. LEAD-301 teaches applied social-media content practice, whereas LEAD-303 interprets literature and intersectional theory. LEAD-304 explicitly emphasizes practical conflict resolution. LEAD-305 and LEAD-307 combine applied community or access-technology practice with substantial legal/social context. Dialogue courses remain human-context on their actual cultural and communication content.

PSYC-300, CRIM-489 and LEAD-308 are **source-inspected but unclassified**: their generic cards leave current topics unspecified. Readable generic content closes this metadata-reading task but cannot establish a particular offering's syllabus. No other actual source-access boundary remains in this batch.
