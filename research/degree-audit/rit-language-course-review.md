This conflicts with treating every permitted three-course path as exactly nine credits, despite the route pages' displayed nine-credit totals and six-to-nine language subtotals. Preserve actual course credits, the three-course/consecutive-language selection rule and the source total as separate statements. ## Content and eligibility

All 107 courses are human-context under taxonomy v1: 66 primarily develop human language/communication and 41 primarily interpret cultural, historical or linguistic subjects. Each has an original summary and individual rationale. A primary cross-review caught an initial applications label on the 66 communication courses before integration; those labels were corrected to the established taxonomy without changing any source facts. No computing or AI category is inferred from technical vocabulary or course prefixes. French Society (MLFR-401) explicitly prioritizes spoken-language practice; Hispanic Cultures through Film and Media (MLSP-315) prioritizes critical cultural interpretation despite its language-menu placement. MLJP-451 studies human language linguistically and does not establish computational methods. MLIT-351 allows a comparative paper or creative project, but its organizing purpose remains comparative cinema and cultural interpretation.

Sixty-one records carry an explicit prerequisite, corequisite, placement or instructor-permission statement; null for the others means no separate field was displayed, not open enrollment. Additional conditions in prose remain in notes. In particular:

- MLSP-301's prose names MLSP 201 or placement, while its formal prerequisite field names MLSP-202/202T or placement score two/equivalent. Both statements remain and their conflict is explicit.
- MLSP-310 labels its rule **Corequisites**. That label survives observation and publication.
- MLJP-410 and MLJP-415 preserve the printed alternative structure including `MLJP-302 and (MLJP-315 or MLJP-310)`. It is not flattened into a single prior course.
- MLJP-310 requires instructor permission; MLJP-315 displays no separate prerequisite field. Both practical courses say students must continue the sequential intermediate courses to advance.
- MLJP-201 is not open to students with prior instruction. Other first-course placement requirements and Russian coordinator consultation remain explicit.
- MLSP-305 requires an approved application and program fee for its one-week community experience. No approval or availability is presumed.
- ENGL-418 requires first-year writing and permits two offerings/six credits only with different topics. Its Russian/Spanish route-specific author/topic limitations remain separate.
- ARTH-549 and HIST-402 vary by topic and prohibit repetition of the same topic. ANTH-255's region is offering-specific; the route's regional condition still applies.
- Several advanced II courses formally allow placement or the intermediate II course; an advanced I prerequisite is not invented. MLIT-302's title says II while its prose calls it first in the intermediate sequence; this inconsistency is recorded.

This contribution creates no identity groups, no new universal AI requirements and no independent whole-degree certification. It supplies metadata for existing route identities; the primary owner integrates exact local selection rules and maintains the global queue.
