# RIT full immersion route queue — 2026-10-11

The [public directory](https://www.rit.edu/study/immersions-and-minors) contains
77 exact immersion titles and links. All are recorded in
`research/rit-immersion-route-queue.json`; a confirmed route title is not a reviewed
curriculum. Two routes currently have complete local rule and course-description
reviews, comprising 51 route-course observations. A further 36 route rules (15 STEM, 13 humanities and eight language routes)
are now reviewed without course-popup expansion. The remaining 39 need local
rules read before expanding their eligible course records. The total distinct course
inventory is **unknown**, not 51. This work remains in progress.

The [university immersion rule](https://www.rit.edu/generaleducation/immersions)
requires three related general-education courses/nine hours, SIS declaration and
no overlap with program requirements. Programs cannot restrict students to a
particular immersion or group. The [student FAQ](https://www.rit.edu/generaleducation/faqs-students)
adds hosting-department approval for some capacity-limited routes and consultation
with the offering department for unavailable-course substitutions. These are
conditional routes, not presumed permission or contradictory requirements.
Minor credit/residence/overlap requirements on the combined directory apply to
minors and are not imported into immersion rules.

The directory defines the observed published route set. It does not close the
university's open choice rule, certify all future approved routes, or make the
two observed branches a complete degree inventory. Each branch contribution
must preserve required, elective-choice and preparation roles, published amounts,
local exclusions and approval conditions. Arbitrary free electives and recursive
prerequisite universes are outside this queue.

Genuine access boundaries remain the SIS login, an invalid published public-SIS
search link and the previously encountered academiccatalog course-search robots
202 response. The ordinary public study pages are independently accessible and
render their curriculum tables in Chrome. Their initial HTTP identity shells
are not curriculum observations. No restricted route is retried for this queue.

`rit_immersion_directory.parse` observes actual main-content route links only;
it emits neither requirements nor audit/category reviews. The exact cached
public-directory replay returns 77 identities. Tests distinguish minor/external
links, access pages and empty shells. Subsequent route review contributions are
separate from this identity observation.
