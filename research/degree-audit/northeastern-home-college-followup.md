# Northeastern home-college applicability follow-up

Reviewed 2026-10-11 for Sociology, Criminal Justice, Journalism and Speech-Language Pathology/Audiology combined BS records. This is an active follow-up to the earlier Business/Biology/Design review. The ordinary public pages below were fetched through the normal robots-aware client, with timestamps/hashes retained in ignored evidence. No applicant/student account or private degree audit was accessed.

## What the public evidence establishes

[Admissions' combined-major explanation](https://admissions.northeastern.edu/academics/combined-majors/) explicitly distinguishes associated colleges, which house constituent courses, from the single college awarding an individual applicant's degree. It directs applicants to their Application Status Check for that designation. Its concrete entries identify Sociology and Criminal Justice with Khoury/CSSH, Journalism with Khoury/CAMD, and Speech-Language Pathology/Audiology with Khoury/Bouvé. These associations are not a published unconditional menu of home-college assignments.

[Khoury's current advising policies](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/current-undergraduate-students/undergraduate-advising-academic-support/academic-policies-and-procedures/) provide additional actionable conditional information: current combined-major students whose home college is elsewhere must meet their Khoury secondary adviser to request Khoury as home college; a student can remain in an existing home college if that college offers the intended combined major, with adviser confirmation. The page describes internal-change academic eligibility and adviser review. It does not list the permitted assignment matrix for each of the four exact degree/cohort records, or declare that every associated college is always available to every student.

[Khoury academic support](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/current-undergraduate-students/undergraduate-advising-academic-support/) identifies the student-specific myPAWS audit as the official requirements for the entering year. Its linked [registrar knowledge article](https://service.northeastern.edu/registrar?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB000019923) returned HTTP success but only an empty client-rendered shell through the public research client. That is insufficient readable public content, not a robots denial or proof the information is unpublished. No private audit was opened.

The [Sociology department catalog](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/undergraduate/social-sciences-humanities/sociology-anthropology/) explicitly lists AI and Sociology under its BS offerings and links to the same Khoury-hosted catalog degree. Its college context and program listing establish departmental participation, not a student's home designation. The [Khoury Sociology marketing page](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/programs/bs-in-artificial-intelligence-and-sociology/) is internally inconsistent: its introduction names CAMD, while its partner section identifies CSSH. Admissions and the department support CSSH participation. This marketing inconsistency is not resolved by changing the degree's award or assigning a home college.

## Additional current advising rules and source scope

The current Khoury advising page states a 2.000 graduation GPA in major CS/CY/AI courses as well as overall, adds later-term major-GPA criteria, and describes major dismissal for a sub-2.000 major GPA from the third term onward. The [2026–27 Khoury college catalog](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/undergraduate/computer-information-science/) states overall 2.000 for graduation and lacks those later-term major-GPA/dismissal clauses. Both pages were readable. The catalog does not expressly waive these additional rules: omission alone is not a contradiction. The advising rules may supplement the catalog. Their effective cohort and exact applicability to students in each allowed home college need confirmation; the research does not silently impose them on every variant or declare them superseded. The first-year major-GPA clause and plan/credit-load conditions remain present in the catalog.

[CSSH's catalog](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/undergraduate/social-sciences-humanities/) follows university progression standards, requires graduation clearance, and limits its Experiential Liberal Arts course requirement to majors or combined majors entirely within CSSH. These cross-college AI combinations are therefore not assigned that additional ELA pool merely because CSSH participates. The [CAMD catalog](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/undergraduate/arts-media-design/) follows university progression and refers program-specific conditions to individual pages, requires clearance, and limits additional BA rules to BA students. No BA rules are assigned to the Journalism BS.

[Bouvé's policy page](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/undergraduate/health-sciences/academic-policies-procedures/) contains consequential conditions: program-specific minimum grades; probation contract deadlines and reviews; dismissal after twice failing the required minimum in the same required course; up to two provisional semesters after major dismissal; and clinical-site requirements when applicable. Its professional conduct provisions specifically name all Communication Sciences and Disorders programs. The precise applicability of college-level versus department-level provisions to the combined Speech/Audiology program, including students with another home college, remains part of the question. A clinically themed course does not itself establish a required placement or all site-specific clearance obligations.
