- **CHEM 117 is mixed human context/applications.** The course explicitly combines historical research harms, race and health outcomes with genomics, biological mechanisms and new biomedical interventions such as personalized medicine. Neither component is clearly subordinate. Its approved-pool provenance does not determine the taxonomy.
- **CHEM 151 is applications.** The description is organized around discovering, synthesizing, modifying and using molecules. Society is the context for those chemical effects, without a separately stated social-analysis method or objective.
- CHEM 11/4/6C/6CH teach atomic, molecular and general chemical principles. Preparatory status does not mean computer-science foundations. CHEM 6CH explicitly references 6C's topics at greater pace/level; both complete blocks are guarded.
- CHEM 171/172/173/174 teach environmental, atmospheric or marine chemical processes. Climate, societal perturbations, resources and energy risks do not alone establish a distinct social-policy curriculum. CHEM 111 addresses biogeochemical evolution and detection of life, not cultural history.
- CHEM 145 teaches biofuel/renewable-material chemistry and production/analysis. CHEM 143C teaches concrete organic-compound identification techniques, rather than unspecified research topics.
