## Scope decisions and remaining qualifications

- **Advising:** semester/term registration and appointment conditions are procedures. They add no course or credit obligation. The full appointment, drop-in, email and business-hours content was inspected; no person was contacted.
- **Core overlap:** the prohibition is exactly the three-way Great Issues / Culture-Diversity / General Education overlap. Other multiple-use permissions remain department- and degree-specific; this page does not grant blanket overlap.
- **Credit raise:** over 18 fall/spring or nine summer credits requires an advisor-approved request; eligibility includes 3.0 cumulative GPA and successful completion of the applicable maximum load. The holistic decision is neither guaranteed nor appealable. Requests exceeding 21 credits go to the Director of Academic Advising. No absolute approved maximum is invented.
- **Repeat:** the three-attempt limit concerns non-repeatable courses and includes W withdrawals. A fourth and final attempt requires documented extenuating circumstances, a success plan, Director review and a University-rule waiver before a registration override. It is not an automatic fourth opportunity. Course/degree-specific minimum grades and GPA replacement formulas are not supplied here.
- **P/NP:** P means C- or better, N below C-. Both the 20%-of-120 credit limit and two-course Fall–Summer academic-year limit apply. Previously letter-graded courses cannot elect P/NP. The page explicitly permits free electives and uses the unusual literal label **“5000-level general education requirement”**, while also excluding specified departmental and Science Core requirements. The level label is not silently corrected to 50000; its exception is retained without broadening it. Placement-based lower language credit requires the advanced course to be letter graded. Abroad-only P/NP transcripts invoke an unspecified Purdue letter-grade calculation. The all-requirement Pass exemption applies **only to Spring 2020**.
- **Math exams:** the title and FAQ are summer-specific, while the opening statement says all Purdue Math courses starting Summer 2026. The contribution retains both contexts; it does not independently certify year-round applicability. Online/distance MA sections and finals are explicitly included. Approved in-person centers, student booking/payment, instructor windows, DRC coordination, and multiple-location arrangements are preserved. The $35 Purdue Testing Services amount is a dated example, not a universal or permanent fee. NCTA certification is a recommendation; international centers must communicate in English. No accommodation or location waiver is presumed.
- **External summer math:** equivalency/advisor review remains conditional. Official transcript submission and prerequisite posting are required; the page warns processing can take seven weeks and block registration for a dependent fall course. No automatic equivalence or transfer-credit award is inferred.
