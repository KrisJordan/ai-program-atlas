# Caltech current undergraduate catalog review

Reviewed 11 October 2026. **No qualifying program found** within the documented current catalog/directory scope. This is a dated, bounded search result, not a claim that Caltech offers no AI education or that an unlisted/future award cannot exist. The [survey record](../data/surveys/caltech-catalog-review-2026-10-11.json) contains citations; the [manifest](caltech-catalog-review-2026-10-11.json) preserves fetched timestamps and content hashes without copied articles.

## Review scope and method

The current catalog navigation identifies 2026–27. Starting from its Graduation Requirements table of contents, inspected all 32 pages explicitly labeled Option or Minor, plus the complete admissions major/minor directory. Read all program and subsection headings, searched complete article text for AI/Artificial Intelligence/GenAI/GeoAI and track/concentration/specialization labels, and reviewed the resulting contexts. All 33 ordinary public pages were successfully retrieved with the project's robots-aware Fetcher and per-host pacing. No access restriction was encountered or bypassed. This is title/identity discovery, not a curriculum extraction or completeness review of these nonqualifying programs.

The catalog's mechanical engineering page defers track definitions to the department. Followed its public undergraduate page to the linked June 2026 Advanced Engineering Electives PDF. Its eight pre-approved tracks are Design, Systems, and Mechatronics; Dynamics and Control; Fluids and Transport; Robotics; Solids and Materials; Structures; Thermodynamics and Energy; and Thesis. None contains AI. Its custom approval route does not establish an AI-titled credential.

## Near matches and interpretation

- Electrical Engineering has a formal **Intelligent Systems** track; preserve the actual title rather than renaming it AI. The other four published EE tracks likewise do not qualify.
- The **Robotics** minor includes an intelligence course group. A course-group label does not rename the minor or establish a formal AI concentration.
- **Computation and Neural Systems** describes AI career applications. **Information and Data Sciences** includes machine-learning courses. Neither official title qualifies.
- Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Environmental Science and Engineering, and Geological and Planetary Sciences publish named tracks; their current names do not qualify. History allows a student-selected area of study, without an established AI-titled offering.
- Engineering and Applied Science and Interdisciplinary Studies permit individually approved study proposals. Their existence does not verify an approved AI-titled program; no individual student proposal is inferred.

The source contains unrelated course-level AI topics, which are outside the award/title scope. Supplemental search results were not used as proof of absence. No launch dates, curricula or program records were invented.
