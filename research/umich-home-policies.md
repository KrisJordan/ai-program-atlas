# Michigan HCAI: home-college minor policies

Reviewed 2026-10-11. This bounded review covers the ten approving home colleges other than LSA, whose policies the coordinator reviewed separately. The UMSI HCAI handbook supplies eligibility; policies below apply conditionally to students in the named home college. They do not replace HCAI's all-residential coursework, letter-grade or GPA rules. BSI has its own handbook conditions. No complete inventory or unrestricted overlap is inferred from missing home-college rules.
