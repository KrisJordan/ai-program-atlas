# Miami contextual course-description review

The four concrete Miami degree records share a large dated cognate inventory. This is a finite content-review queue, not a required-course total. Current official course metadata does not establish that every listed cognate candidate is eligible for a particular degree, student or term. Primary-table fixed/choice roles and all cognate conditional ancestry remain unchanged.

## First complete-block batch — 2026-10-11

Read 42 complete official course blocks: 23 already reviewed in another Miami degree context, plus 19 newly inspected business/economics records. These reads support **131 previously unreviewed program-course observations**, bringing the four records to **331 reviewed observations**. The exact remaining queue is **1,849 course codes / 7,396 observations**, recorded in [the running queue](miami-description-review-remaining.json); it is accessible work still in progress, not an access blocker.

The new blocks include the [Business](https://bulletin.miami.edu/courses-az/bus/), [Economics](https://bulletin.miami.edu/courses-az/eco/), [Business Law](https://bulletin.miami.edu/courses-az/bsl/), [Finance](https://bulletin.miami.edu/courses-az/fin/) and [Marketing](https://bulletin.miami.edu/courses-az/mkt/) chapters. Economic analysis of markets, growth, energy and regulation is substantive economics domain content; history of economic thought is human context. Business ethics/legal institutions are human context; corporate sustainability combines management practice and institutional obligations and remains mixed. No course becomes mathematics merely because its prerequisite is calculus, or AI because it appears in an AI degree.

Important actual eligibility findings remain explicit: BUS 200 requires nonbusiness majors and offers general-education credit; BUS 201 excludes business major/minor credit; FIN 300 excludes business students’ credit and explicitly requires nonbusiness majors. BUS 206 describes a nonbusiness audience without stating an equivalent enrollment prohibition. These findings qualify existing contextual membership; they do not silently remove dated inventory facts or manufacture a waiver. Similar ECO 301/303 titles and MKT 201/301 exclusions do not establish cross-listed identities.

The overlap batch was reread rather than blindly inheriting a prior tag. For example, CSC 506 retains mixed logic/automated-reasoning content in the BA cognate context but remains absent from the BA primary-table option pool. MAS 311 retains its concurrent calculus alternative. Null prerequisite fields are not assertions that enrollment is unrestricted.

`miami_context_course_reviews.py` observes exact course identities, complete-block digests, published credits, original summaries and source eligibility. The observer composes with earlier source parsers while preserving their requirements and unrelated courses. It never emits a manual review or changes role ancestry. Changed chapter identity, content, eligibility or credit amount fails for review. Registered source notes distinguish current public course chapters from a degree/cohort assignment: the site contains current copyright/download references and a stale PDF label, so navigation alone is not used to invent a course-catalog cohort.
