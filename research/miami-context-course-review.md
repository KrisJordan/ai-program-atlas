`miami_context_course_reviews.py` observes exact course identities, complete-block digests, published credits, original summaries and source eligibility. The observer composes with earlier source parsers while preserving their requirements and unrelated courses. It never emits a manual review or changes role ancestry. Changed chapter identity, content, eligibility or credit amount fails for review. Registered source notes distinguish current public course chapters from a degree/cohort assignment: the site contains current copyright/download references and a stale PDF label, so navigation alone is not used to invent a course-catalog cohort.
