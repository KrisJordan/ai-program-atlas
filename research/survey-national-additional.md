# Additional National University survey — 2026-10-10

25 previously pending institutions were selected from the rank ≤60 working cohort. Cohort membership/rank is inherited from the separate cohort evidence and is not reverified here. This file does not claim complete institution coverage or program absence.

## Method and limits

Read official public HTML and relevant headings, program links and nearby context. Direct requests checked robots rules, used normal TLS verification and bounded response sizes/timeouts. Raw pages are temporary files under `/tmp`, not repository content. HTTP 403 / inaccessible robots preflights were respected. Moved 404 routes were resolved through official navigation. Two discoveries meet the title/kind test; 19 institutions have partial inspections and four are inaccessible. Certificates, graduate tracks, courses and optional areas of interest are not silently converted to undergraduate degrees/minors/concentrations.
