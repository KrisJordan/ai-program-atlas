# Shared Architecture evidence

The four Miami degrees share the [50-code Architecture contribution](../../../research/degree-audit/miami-architecture-context-review.md), with its observer and manual manifest at `atlas/adapters/miami_arc_context_reviews.*`. It distinguishes technical design practice from architectural history, retains variable credits and exact preparation, and leaves two special investigations unclassified. Same-title Palladio courses are not merged. Program owners integrate the existing contextual records without changing required or choice membership.
