# Shared Art History evidence

The four Miami degrees share the 50-code [Art History contribution](../../../research/degree-audit/miami-art-history-context-review.md), implemented in `atlas/adapters/miami_arh_context_reviews.py` and its adjacent manifest. Manual judgments separate historical interpretation from museum practice and preserve four unknown subjects, one mixed subject and an explicit ARH 326 title/description scope mismatch. The program owner integrates facts and reviews without changing contextual membership. This batch does not certify a full degree audit.
