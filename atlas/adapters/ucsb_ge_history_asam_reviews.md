# UCSB History and Asian American Studies: bounded GE content review

Reviewed 2026-10-11 against the previously downloaded, official 2026–2027 course-search CSV. The source is the [catalog's D/E/F/G result set](https://catalog.ucsb.edu/courses?generalSubjectAreas=D&generalSubjectAreas=E&generalSubjectAreas=F&generalSubjectAreas=G&page=1&cq=). No network requests were made after the catalog's human-verification boundary.

All 97 assigned complete rows were read: HIST 56, HIST W 6, AS AM 33, AS AMW 2. The review includes every description, fixed and variable credit column, advisor comment, repeat comment, and prerequisite flag. Original summaries and individual classification rationales are in the JSON manifest. Ninety-six observations concern historical, social, or cultural interpretation; AS AM 79 develops playwriting, staging, acting and directing, and maps to domain applications. Historical study of science or technology does not become a computing or natural-science course.

Exact prerequisite fields are absent from this export. Neither Yes nor No is converted to a prerequisite rule or an absence conclusion. Specific visible restrictions and recommendations remain in notes. HIST 2CH explicitly requires concurrent HIST 2C in its description; it describes the companion as four units, whereas HIST 2C's fixed-unit column says five. The honors seminar's own one-unit value and the companion's five-unit value remain separate, and the discrepancy remains explicit. HIST 8A before 8B and AS AM 156's junior/senior audience are recommendations, not mandatory prerequisites.

The sparse descriptions for HIST 159C and 184A/B are summarized only at their stated scope. HIST 133C's description dates the endpoint of unification to 1989; the summary attributes that date to the source instead of quietly changing it. Source credit exclusions, same-course statements, and online-version statements remain visible. They do not automatically create reciprocal cross-list groups or alias records. In particular, the online AS AMW 1 row does not inherit the in-person AS AM 1 row's American History and Institutions attribute.

The parser first validates all 856 pool rows, then the exact membership and full-row hashes of these 97 observations. It emits bounded facts, never manual category-review certifications, full-degree audit status, requirement membership, or identity groups. Changed or missing rows require a fresh human review. Raw catalog descriptions remain in the ignored private cache.
