# Data exchange v1

Researchers own data/programs/<slug>.json and atlas/adapters/<slug>/ (adapter.py plus NOTES.md), plus tests specific to adapter. Core schema, database, CLI and networking owned by coordinator. Frontend owns atlas/site.py, atlas/web/, tests/e2e/.

Each program JSON: {id, institution: {id,name,aliases:[],calendar:"semester|quarter|other|unknown",website}, title, award_level:"BS|BA|BSE|BSAI|minor|concentration|other", program_kind:"degree|minor|concentration|additional_major", hosting_unit, status:"active|announced|discontinued|unknown", first_offered:null or {value,precision,evidence_url,uncertainty}, overview, catalog_year, verification_status:"confirmed|partial", last_verified:"YYYY-MM-DD", curriculum_status:"complete|partial|pending", links:[{kind:"overview|catalog|study|outcomes|news",url,label}], sources:[{id,url,title,fetched_at,source_date:null,catalog_year,locator,confidence:"high|medium|low"}], outcomes:[{text,kind:"source_stated|inferred_taxonomy",source_id,locator}], courses:[{id,code,name,description:null,prerequisites:null,credits:null,credit_unit:"semester_hours|quarter_hours|units|course_units|unknown",category,source_id,url,public_site:null,syllabus:null}], requirements:[{id,parent_id:null,title,kind:"all|choose_courses|choose_credits|alternative|note",count:null,credits:null,credit_unit,category,source_id,source_text:null,summary,course_ids:[]}], notes:[] }.

Use null for unknown values. Source text must be minimal; summarize descriptions rather than reproduce catalogs. IDs stable lowercase hyphens. Categories: foundations, mathematics, ai_ml, systems, human_context, applications, electives, other. Published credit amounts may be numeric with credit_unit unknown when the source unit is not normalized; do not total or compare these. Preserve the source wording in notes. Known unit conversions must never be guessed. Arbitrary nested requirements via parent_id. A course may be listed in multiple groups; do not double-count it. If curriculum not fully extracted say partial. Outcome taxonomy is inferred and separate from source statements. Every adapter exports parse(html: str) -> dict, with official URL(s) in SOURCES constant; it must actually extract/validate official headings or curriculum, not pretend a static fixture is fresh evidence. Coordinator provides safe fetch and persistence.

Public site export: {meta:{generated_at,cohort,coverage_summary,limitations},institutions:[{id,name,aliases,calendar,website,coverage_status,coverage_note,rankings:[],program_ids:[]}],programs:[program JSON],rankings:[],csrankings:{methodology,observations:[]}}. Frontend build(data: dict, output: pathlib.Path, base_path: str='/ai-program-atlas/') -> None writes HTML/JSON/CSS/JS; static program paths programs/<id>/index.html plus root index.html, coverage/index.html, methodology/index.html; comparison/filter query state at root. No server runtime. Escape all source text.

Curriculum completeness requires the explicit reviewer audit and scope rules in [CURRICULUM_REVIEW.md](CURRICULUM_REVIEW.md). This optional `curriculum_audit` object is mandatory for `complete` records and is persisted with relational source references. Scrapers must not emit review audits or promote records to complete.

Ranking evidence preserves `source_date`, `fetched_at`, and `locator`. When an official source verifies top-100 membership but omits the exact rank, use `rank: null`, `rank_upper_bound: 100`, and `verification_status: primary_membership_verified`; never substitute 100 as the exact rank. This still requires explicit edition/category evidence. Inferred editions must use a separate qualified verification label and a research note. Neither membership bounds nor newly observed ties certify the complete cohort.

`meta.curriculum_summary` reports complete/partial/pending inventory counts, audit count, program-course records, requirement nodes, and available course metadata. These are research-progress counts, not unique institutional courses, required-course totals, or quality scores.

Program families are optional, explicit research records in `data/families/`. See [PROGRAM_FAMILIES.md](PROGRAM_FAMILIES.md) for the shared requirement/binding/override contract. Program source JSON contains local requirements plus ID-only bindings; public export contains fully resolved requirements and their origin, keeping existing consumers and URLs stable. The export also adds `families` and `meta.family_summary`. A complete inherited record's audit must pin `family_content_hash`; family updates and their reviewed variants are atomic. Never infer family membership or AI+X orientation from title text.

Outcome entries may specify `statement_type: learning_outcome|program_aim|career_pathway|unspecified`, independently of provenance `kind`. Use explicit source context to classify; an admissions career list is a career pathway, not demonstrated learning or employment evidence. Missing type stays unspecified. Do not classify from keywords or promote a program aim into an assessed learning outcome. The relational outcomes table preserves this distinction.

Title scope includes the explicit AI compounds GenAI and GeoAI when the official source establishes their artificial-intelligence meaning. Preserve the official title and explain the expansion in source notes; do not rename the credential to force a match. Incidental letter sequences, generic computing titles and AI electives alone remain insufficient.

`program_kind="concentration"` also covers a catalog-defined track, emphasis, specialization or formally defined general-education immersion with an official AI title and explicit curricular/declaration rules, even when the institution does not record it as a separate transcript credential. Preserve the institution's exact title and terminology; state its host degree and transcript/credential limitations prominently in the overview and notes with evidence. A mandatory declared area such as Rice BSECE's AI & Systems specialization can qualify on that basis. RIT's explicitly defined undergraduate general-education concentrations called immersions can also qualify; preserve the immersion title and distinguish it from a minor or standalone award. Do not infer transcript recognition or its absence. An informal interest area, research theme, elective suggestion or certificate does not become a concentration merely because it has an AI label. Scoped completeness covers the documented path and its applicable local policies, not the full host degree.

Curriculum comparison uses `atlas.curriculum_profile.requirement_profile` on the **resolved** program graph, after family inheritance and explicit overrides. `required` counts stable course IDs on an `all` node with an all-only ancestry; it is the fixed mapped set, not every course a student must select. `options` counts IDs directly in a choice or an `all` descendant of a choice, excluding IDs independently in the fixed set. A `note` ancestor prevents either classification; contextual and unbound records remain `unresolved`. All three sets are disjoint. Deduplicate by stable ID, or an explicitly source-reviewed cross-listing in course_identity_groups; never infer identity from names, slashes, ordinary substitutions or credit exclusions. All source code records remain available. Course taxonomy is independent from requirement membership: an elective can be AI, and a required course can remain `other`.

Choice descriptors preserve source counts, credits, units and nesting context (`required`, `conditional`, `unresolved`). Never add these descriptors into a total: nested routes, overlays and overlap rules may share courses. Never convert option-pool breadth into actual elective load, combine course counts with credits, promote every observed option because count equals pool size, or select an example path. The profile deliberately does not solve student-specific waiver, cohort or prose conditions. Its denominator is distinct documented course records in the displayed set, not a percentage of the whole degree. A zero fixed count means no fixed set was established from this graph; required choice obligations and unresolved records remain visible. Source-stated loads stay beside their exact choice rule and evidence.

Major award filters use `atlas.awards.major_award_buckets`: exact `BS`, `BSE` and `BA` values map directly; other labels remain Other/unspecified unless separately reviewed. Do not infer BSE from an engineering host, normalize abbreviations by prefix, or turn an additional major into a standalone award. All additional-major records remain separately labeled within Majors and use Other/unspecified. Distinct cataloged BA and BS curricula retain distinct program IDs.

A genuinely combined degree record may optionally have `award_options: [{label, normalized, source_id, locator}]`, with the exact source label, a normalized `BS|BSE|BA|other` bucket, registered program evidence and a nonempty evidence locator for each option. Only standalone degree records may provide this field. Multiple explicitly verified options participate in every applicable filter but count as one program in aggregate results. Migration 009 stores these mappings relationally; import, export and revision history preserve their original labels and evidence. Empty/missing options do not establish an equivalence. No existing unusual label is reclassified automatically.

Default directory order is ascending numerical U.S. News rank within each institution category, program-type tab and award filter. `atlas.rankings.institution_rank_context` uses only the requested edition/category and a positive integer with cited `primary_verified`, `enumerated_secondary` or `secondary_reported` provenance. Secondary evidence remains explicitly secondary; sorting does not certify primary enumeration. Inferred editions, missing or unverified ranks, membership bounds and conflicting numbers sort last. Never turn a top-100 bound into rank 100 or combine category ranks. Equal ranks use institution name, program/family title and stable ID for deterministic alphabetical display, without changing the published tied rank. Families use their institution's rank. User-selected alphabetical order persists as `sort=name`; static/no-JS output uses the same default ranking keys.


See [degree audit identity contract](DEGREE_AUDIT.md) for optional course_identity_groups and migration 012. Profiles retain source_record_count alongside the deduplicated mapped-identity count. Canonical course classification is evidence-checked; conflicting reviewed subjects reject the identity mapping. No credit totals or selection quantities are changed.

## Parent programs and second-major eligibility

Second-major-only status is independent of the degree award. A source-confirmed `additional_major` may carry `major_relationship: {kind: "second_major_only", official_term, eligibility, source_ids, locator, reviewed_at}`. Preserve the institution's own additional-major, second-major or co-major terminology and entry restrictions. This relationship produces the `second_major` navigation bucket but never changes `award_level`. An unspecified award, optional double-major compatibility or hosting unit does not establish this relationship. Legacy records lacking evidence remain in their actual award bucket.

A formal concentration/track record can carry `parent_programs: [{id, title, award_level, award_label, relationship, parent_program_id, eligibility, source_ids, locator, reviewed_at}]`. Each local relationship ID is stable; `relationship` is `concentration`, `track`, `specialization`, `emphasis` or `immersion`. Parent titles and exact award labels are source verified. Unknown awards, optional linked Atlas parent IDs and unknown eligibility are null. Multiple actual parent programs/awards are separate entries; an optional double major is not another parent. A hosting unit or course prefix never establishes a parent.

`parent_program_review: {status: "verified|partial|unknown", reviewed_at, source_ids, notes: []}` records the bounded review, including a university-wide path with no one verified parent. Empty/missing relationships display an explicit unknown parent, not an inferred degree. A verified review requires at least one named parent with known award; partial permits verified names with unresolved awards or remaining alternatives. Every source ID references a registered program source. Migration 013 stores these relationships and their evidence in normalized tables; original records and revisions retain exact reviewed text. Scrape observations cannot certify these manual reviews, and source failures leave them intact.

Course records may carry `notes: [string, ...]` for ordered course-specific source qualifications, conditional eligibility and catalog-version context. Nonempty strings are required; missing/empty arrays mean no recorded note. These notes have always survived canonical snapshots, revision history and public JSON. Migration 015 additionally backfills a relational `course_notes` table with stable per-course order without modifying the canonical hashes/history. Source provenance remains on the course and its registered evidence; notes do not certify a new independent review. Detail pages display escaped, keyboard-accessible expandable notes beside the course description.

An official source may name a course without publishing its code. Preserve `code: null` with the source-stated nonempty `name`, a stable safe-slug course `id` and registered `source_id`; never invent a code or infer identity from the title. The `code` key is explicit and known codes remain nonempty strings. Migration 016 makes the relational code nullable while preserving child relationships, canonical snapshots, revision history and manual overrides. Requirement membership and profile counts use stable IDs independently of codes. Family shared requirements defined by `course_codes` require reviewed known codes in each binding; a title-only record cannot satisfy such a binding by inference.

## Independent additional-major reviews

Additional majors have a separate evidence ledger in `data/additional-major-audit.json`, compiled from `research/additional-major-review-*.json` with `atlas additional-audit-report`. Each row carries `scope: additional_major`, an explicit `scope_summary`, reviewer/date, the three bounded core dimensions, and a separate `eligibility_review` using the same status/summary/evidence/limitations shape. Manual expected identity sets and category counts are checked against the represented graph; unchecked totals remain null. The digest from `atlas.additional_major_audit.review_fingerprint` pins core facts plus title, award, program kind and second-major eligibility. Changes invalidate the review rather than inheriting certification.

Ingestion validates this ledger atomically and stores it in SQLite metadata; export adds `meta.additional_major_audit` and the matching `program.additional_major_audit`. Its totals never enter the standalone-degree audit. Detail pages expose source links, limitations and second-major eligibility, explicitly excluding whole-host-degree certification. A missing ledger row remains not reviewed. Successful scraping and software tests do not establish independent evidence review.

## CSRankings research snapshots

`csrankings` exports `methodology`, `observations`, `full_us_results` and `population`. Five individual fields plus `aggregate` are explicit for every roster institution. `value` is the upstream displayed score, not raw publication count; `adjusted_count` preserves fractional counts for individual fields, and aggregate `component_counts` preserves all five inputs. Unknown scores/ranks/counts are null. Missingness is `observed`, `no_selected_output`, `not_listed`, `outside_eligible`, `unmatched` or `source_missing`; absence from the registry does not establish eligibility. Full U.S. competition ranks precede roster filtering. Configuration preserves years, geography, exact field/venue membership, snapshot, hashes, method, permission statement and attribution. Migration017 retains immutable reviewed artifacts. Invalid/partial input rolls back atomically; repeat ingestion creates no new revision. Relational values must agree with the artifact at export.
