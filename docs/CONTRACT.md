# Data exchange v1

Researchers own data/programs/<slug>.json and atlas/adapters/<slug>/ (adapter.py plus NOTES.md), plus tests specific to adapter. Core schema, database, CLI and networking owned by coordinator. Frontend owns atlas/site.py, atlas/web/, tests/e2e/.

Each program JSON: {id, institution: {id,name,aliases:[],calendar:"semester|quarter|other|unknown",website}, title, award_level:"BS|BA|BSE|BSAI|minor|concentration|other", program_kind:"degree|minor|concentration|additional_major", hosting_unit, status:"active|announced|discontinued|unknown", first_offered:null or {value,precision,evidence_url,uncertainty}, overview, catalog_year, verification_status:"confirmed|partial", last_verified:"YYYY-MM-DD", curriculum_status:"complete|partial|pending", links:[{kind:"overview|catalog|study|outcomes|news",url,label}], sources:[{id,url,title,fetched_at,source_date:null,catalog_year,locator,confidence:"high|medium|low"}], outcomes:[{text,kind:"source_stated|inferred_taxonomy",source_id,locator}], courses:[{id,code,name,description:null,prerequisites:null,credits:null,credit_unit:"semester_hours|quarter_hours|units|course_units|unknown",category,source_id,url,public_site:null,syllabus:null}], requirements:[{id,parent_id:null,title,kind:"all|choose_courses|choose_credits|alternative|note",count:null,credits:null,credit_unit,category,source_id,source_text:null,summary,course_ids:[]}], notes:[] }.

Use null for unknown values. Source text must be minimal; summarize descriptions rather than reproduce catalogs. IDs stable lowercase hyphens. Categories: foundations, mathematics, ai_ml, systems, human_context, applications, electives, other. Published credit amounts may be numeric with credit_unit unknown when the source unit is not normalized; do not total or compare these. Preserve the source wording in notes. Known unit conversions must never be guessed. Arbitrary nested requirements via parent_id. A course may be listed in multiple groups; do not double-count it. If curriculum not fully extracted say partial. Outcome taxonomy is inferred and separate from source statements. Every adapter exports parse(html: str) -> dict, with official URL(s) in SOURCES constant; it must actually extract/validate official headings or curriculum, not pretend a static fixture is fresh evidence. Coordinator provides safe fetch and persistence.

Public site export: {meta:{generated_at,cohort,coverage_summary,limitations},institutions:[{id,name,aliases,calendar,website,coverage_status,coverage_note,rankings:[],program_ids:[]}],programs:[program JSON],rankings:[],csrankings:{methodology,observations:[]}}. Frontend build(data: dict, output: pathlib.Path, base_path: str='/ai-program-atlas/') -> None writes HTML/JSON/CSS/JS; static program paths programs/<id>/index.html plus root index.html, coverage/index.html, methodology/index.html; comparison/filter query state at root. No server runtime. Escape all source text.

Curriculum completeness requires the explicit reviewer audit and scope rules in [CURRICULUM_REVIEW.md](CURRICULUM_REVIEW.md). This optional `curriculum_audit` object is mandatory for `complete` records and is persisted with relational source references. Scrapers must not emit review audits or promote records to complete.

Ranking evidence preserves `source_date`, `fetched_at`, and `locator`. When an official source verifies top-100 membership but omits the exact rank, use `rank: null`, `rank_upper_bound: 100`, and `verification_status: primary_membership_verified`; never substitute 100 as the exact rank. This still requires explicit edition/category evidence. Inferred editions must use a separate qualified verification label and a research note. Neither membership bounds nor newly observed ties certify the complete cohort.

`meta.curriculum_summary` reports complete/partial/pending inventory counts, audit count, program-course records, requirement nodes, and available course metadata. These are research-progress counts, not unique institutional courses, required-course totals, or quality scores.

Program families are optional, explicit research records in `data/families/`. See [PROGRAM_FAMILIES.md](PROGRAM_FAMILIES.md) for the shared requirement/binding/override contract. Program source JSON contains local requirements plus ID-only bindings; public export contains fully resolved requirements and their origin, keeping existing consumers and URLs stable. The export also adds `families` and `meta.family_summary`. A complete inherited record's audit must pin `family_content_hash`; family updates and their reviewed variants are atomic. Never infer family membership or AI+X orientation from title text.

Outcome entries may specify `statement_type: learning_outcome|program_aim|career_pathway|unspecified`, independently of provenance `kind`. Use explicit source context to classify; an admissions career list is a career pathway, not demonstrated learning or employment evidence. Missing type stays unspecified. Do not classify from keywords or promote a program aim into an assessed learning outcome. The relational outcomes table preserves this distinction.

Title scope includes the explicit AI compounds GenAI and GeoAI when the official source establishes their artificial-intelligence meaning. Preserve the official title and explain the expansion in source notes; do not rename the credential to force a match. Incidental letter sequences, generic computing titles and AI electives alone remain insufficient.

`program_kind="concentration"` also covers a catalog-defined track, emphasis, specialization or formally defined general-education immersion with an official AI title and explicit curricular/declaration rules, even when the institution does not record it as a separate transcript credential. Preserve the institution's exact title and terminology; state its host degree and transcript/credential limitations prominently in the overview and notes with evidence. A mandatory declared area such as Rice BSECE's AI & Systems specialization can qualify on that basis. RIT's explicitly defined undergraduate general-education concentrations called immersions can also qualify; preserve the immersion title and distinguish it from a minor or standalone award. Do not infer transcript recognition or its absence. An informal interest area, research theme, elective suggestion or certificate does not become a concentration merely because it has an AI label. Scoped completeness covers the documented path and its applicable local policies, not the full host degree.

Curriculum comparison uses `atlas.curriculum_profile.requirement_profile` on the **resolved** program graph, after family inheritance and explicit overrides. `required` counts stable course IDs on an `all` node with an all-only ancestry; it is the fixed mapped set, not every course a student must select. `options` counts IDs directly in a choice or an `all` descendant of a choice, excluding IDs independently in the fixed set. A `note` ancestor prevents either classification; contextual and unbound records remain `unresolved`. All three sets are disjoint. Deduplicate by stable ID, not inferred name/code equivalence. Course taxonomy is independent from requirement membership: an elective can be AI, and a required course can remain `other`.

Choice descriptors preserve source counts, credits, units and nesting context (`required`, `conditional`, `unresolved`). Never add these descriptors into a total: nested routes, overlays and overlap rules may share courses. Never convert option-pool breadth into actual elective load, combine course counts with credits, promote every observed option because count equals pool size, or select an example path. The profile deliberately does not solve student-specific waiver, cohort or prose conditions. Its denominator is distinct documented course records in the displayed set, not a percentage of the whole degree. A zero fixed count means no fixed set was established from this graph; required choice obligations and unresolved records remain visible. Source-stated loads stay beside their exact choice rule and evidence.
